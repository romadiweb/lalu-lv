## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a crafted visit menu for a real handmade destination, not a generic list of activities. It refuses the old long unstructured offer text by making every excursion feel selectable, comparable, and personal while preserving LaLu's slightly fairy-tale countryside character.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white canvas, cream and soft-surface cards, one lavender signature offer card, dark ink typography, 12-24px radii, light hairlines, small authored SVG icons, and the tall-card rhythm suggested by the user's reference image. The cards borrow the reference's confident top band and floating icon tile, translated into LaLu's restrained cream/lavender system.

STORY: Visitors understand that LaLu welcomes groups for workshop visits, Vectēva stāsts, Pagalma piedzīvojumi, creative workshops, countryside food rituals, celebrations, camps, and small picnic/fireplace arrangements. They see which offers are free, priced, or by agreement, and know that booking happens by contacting LaLu directly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky navigation sits above a wide two-column hero. Left side carries the large "Ekskursijas LaLu darbnīcā" headline, concise invitation copy, and two primary actions. Right side is a layered visit menu object with pricing chips, group-use notes, and a compact studio-path timeline so the offer is demonstrated immediately, not deferred below the fold.

FORM: Direct shaped extension; no concept-seed run because the user pinned the page, old-site source, design system, and card reference. Seed key: direct-ekskursijas-2026-10-05. Signature interaction is card-action polish: each offer card has a stable button, icon-only motion, and responsive equal-height comparison without becoming a generic pricing table.
