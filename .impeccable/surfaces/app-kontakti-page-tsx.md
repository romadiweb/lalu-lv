Scope: `/kontakti/` public contact page.
Mode: Persuade.
Audience: families, group organizers, workshop visitors, gift buyers, and people ordering Fantazijas ziedi or handmade LaLu work.
Job: make reaching LaLu feel immediate, trustworthy, and warm while showing that the destination is a real creative workshop in Aizpute.
Action: call, email, open the map, visit LaLu social channels, or start a visit/order conversation.
Proof/content: current lalu.lv contact details, workshop story, visits, events, handmade goods, and Fantazijas ziedi.
Constraints: preserve the existing LaLu visual system, warm canvas, single lavender accent, neutral premium cards, existing header/footer, Latvian copy, and accessible semantic links.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a gracious arrival desk for a handmade studio, not a generic contact form. It refuses the default stack of address, phone, and map by making the first viewport a useful invitation: what LaLu is, where it is, and the fastest ways to begin a visit, order, or workshop conversation.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white canvas, cream cards, lavender only for the primary action and one high-value panel, dark ink type, rounded 12-24px surfaces, light hairlines, and studio-like spacing. The map is framed as a destination object with soft neutral controls around it rather than dropped in as a utility block.

STORY: Visitors understand that LaLu is a personal creative workshop in Aizpute with handmade work, excursions, workshops, events, and Fantazijas ziedi. They can immediately choose a route: use the contact facts, follow LaLu on Facebook or Instagram, open Google Maps for directions, or read what to mention before contacting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky navigation sits above a spacious two-column hero. Left side holds the large "Sazinies ar LaLu" headline, short studio copy, three compact contact facts, and a horizontal row of social icon buttons. Right side holds a large rounded map composition with the address and opening context layered nearby, so the location is visible within seconds.

FORM: Direct shaped extension; no concept-seed run because the user pinned the surface purpose, content, current-site sourcing, and Google Maps integration. Seed key: direct-kontakti-2026-10-05. Signature interaction is quiet map/action affordance polish: clear focus states, lifted action cards, and a responsive map that remains useful on mobile.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
